Questions

Before you book

The five things homeowners ask us most. Call the shop if yours is not here.

Q What does a service call cost?

A diagnostic visit is $89 and covers a full system check with written readings. If you approve a repair on the same visit, the $89 is credited toward it. Repairs are flat-rate from a published book, so an after-hours call costs the same as a Tuesday morning.

Q Do you really answer the phone at night?

Yes — by one of our own technicians, not a call center. On-call rotates weekly and the tech on duty has the schedule and your service history in front of them. No-heat calls below freezing get dispatched immediately.

Q How long does a system replacement take?

Most single-system changeouts are one day, start to finish, with the house warm or cool again that evening. Jobs involving duct modifications or a fuel conversion run two days. We pull the permit and schedule the inspection ourselves.

Q Is financing available?

We offer terms up to 120 months through a lending partner, including a 12-month no-interest option on qualifying installs. Approval takes a few minutes at the kitchen table and does not change the price we quoted.

Q Which brands do you service?

All of them. We install Trane and Bosch, but our techs carry parts and diagnostics for Carrier, Lennox, Goodman, Rheem, American Standard, York and Mitsubishi mini-splits. Being out of warranty does not mean being out of luck.

Need it fixed today?

Book before 11am and we will be there the same day, seven days a week.

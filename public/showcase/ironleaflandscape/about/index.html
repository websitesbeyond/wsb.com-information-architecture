About the studio

Eighteen people, one crew, since 1999.

Ironleaf started as two people with a borrowed truck and a stone order we could not quite afford. The thing that has not changed is that the people who draw the garden are the people who build it.

We work only in the Cascade foothills, between Bellhaven and Ridgeway. That limit is deliberate: it means we know which slopes slide, which soils drain, and whose masons built the wall in the portfolio.

The engagement

How a matter proceeds at Northgate.

You will meet the attorney who handles your file, and you will keep that attorney. We do not pass matters down to whoever is available in the second year.

A plan the family cannot explain to each other is not a plan. Half of this work is drafting; the other half is making sure everyone understands what was drafted. Eleanor R. Vance, Managing Partner

  1. Initial conference

    Ninety minutes, billed at a flat $350 and credited against the engagement if you retain us. You leave with a written summary of your options whether or not you do.

  2. Written engagement letter

    Scope, fee basis, and the name of the responsible attorney, in plain language, before any substantive work begins.

  3. Drafting and review

    Documents arrive with a cover memorandum explaining each provision and why it is there. We expect you to ask about it.

  4. Execution

    Signing conference at our office, with witnesses and a notary on staff. Originals vaulted here; certified copies to you.

  5. Periodic review

    A no-charge review every third year, and whenever the law or your family changes. Most plans need one amendment per decade.

Common questions

Before you call.

What does an initial consultation cost?

A flat $350 for ninety minutes with the attorney who would handle the matter. If you retain the firm within sixty days, that amount is credited against your first invoice.

You will receive a written summary of your options after the conference whether or not you engage us.

How are fees structured?

Estate planning and business formation are quoted as flat fees before work begins. Litigation and administration are billed hourly, between $275 and $450 depending on the attorney, against a retainer stated in the engagement letter.

Invoices itemise every entry. We do not bill for brief telephone calls about an existing matter.

Do I need a trust, or is a will sufficient?

For most Pennsylvania families with a single residence and straightforward beneficiaries, a properly drafted will with durable powers of attorney is sufficient and considerably cheaper to maintain.

Trusts earn their cost where there is real property in more than one state, a beneficiary who should not receive funds outright, or a privacy concern. We will tell you plainly which applies.

Which counties do you practise in?

Harlan, Wessex, and Greenbrier counties, with appearances in the Orphans' Court divisions of each. We accept matters elsewhere in the Commonwealth only on referral from a firm we know.

How long does probate take here?

An uncontested Harlan County estate with no real property to sell typically closes in nine to fourteen months, governed mostly by the inheritance tax clearance rather than by the court.

Contested matters run longer. We give a written estimate of both time and cost at the outset and revise it in writing if it changes.

Will I work with a paralegal or an attorney?

Both, by design. Routine filings and document assembly are handled by our paralegals at a lower rate, but every document is reviewed and every substantive conversation is had by the responsible attorney named in your engagement letter.

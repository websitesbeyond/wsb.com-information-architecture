Our services

Everything a family needs in one practice, from a toddler’s first visit to a crown milled while you wait.

Cleanings & exams

Every six months for most patients, every three or four if we are managing gum disease.

A hygiene visit here is scheduled for forty-five minutes with the same hygienist each time, because somebody who has seen your mouth before notices change faster than a stranger does. Nothing gets rushed to make room for an overbooked chair.

What is included

  • Full-mouth scaling and polish
  • Six-point gum measurements, charted
  • Digital x-rays on a sensible schedule
  • Oral cancer screening every visit
  • Fluoride varnish on request
  • Home-care coaching, not a sales pitch

Fillings & restorations

Composite only. We do not place new amalgam, and we will tell you when an old filling is fine to leave alone.

Small cavities get small fillings. We use tooth-coloured composite matched to your shade, bonded in layers, and we take an intraoral photo before and after so you can actually see the work rather than take our word for it.

Also in this category

  • Replacing failing or leaking fillings
  • Onlays where a filling would be too weak
  • Sealants for molars, adults included
  • Root canal therapy on front and bicuspid teeth
  • Night guards for grinding and clenching
  • Extractions, with specialist referral when warranted

Same-day crowns

Scanned, milled and seated in one appointment — usually about two hours.

The old way meant two appointments, a goopy impression and a temporary crown that came off on a bagel. We scan the tooth, design the crown on screen with you watching, and mill it from a ceramic block in the next room.

Why patients prefer it

  • One visit instead of two, no temporary
  • Digital scan instead of impression trays
  • Shade matched to the neighbouring teeth
  • Full ceramic, no dark metal margin
  • Five-year warranty on the restoration
  • Bite checked and adjusted the same day

Cosmetic care

Conservative by preference — the least tooth removed for the result you want.

We start with the cheapest thing that might work. Often that is whitening and reshaping an edge, not six veneers. If veneers genuinely are the answer, you will see a mock-up on your own photos before we touch anything.

Options

  • In-office whitening, about ninety minutes
  • Custom take-home trays and gel
  • Composite bonding for chips and gaps
  • Porcelain veneers with a digital preview
  • Clear aligner consultations
  • Gum contouring for uneven smile lines

Children & emergencies

First visits from age one. Two urgent slots held open every weekday morning.

A first appointment for a one-year-old takes about fifteen minutes and happens mostly on a parent’s lap. For emergencies, call before 9am and we will almost always see you that day — broken teeth, lost crowns, swelling, and the toothache that started on a Saturday.

Included

  • Age-one through teenage visits
  • Sealants and sports mouthguards
  • Space maintainers and growth monitoring
  • Same-day relief for pain and swelling
  • Re-cementing crowns and bridges
  • Weekend triage by phone for existing patients
What to expect

A first appointment, step by step

About ninety minutes. Here is exactly how it goes.

Paperwork before you arrive

We text a link the day before so you are not filling in a clipboard in the waiting room. It takes about four minutes on a phone.

Photos and x-rays — 15 minutes

A hygienist takes a digital series and intraoral photos. You will see them on the screen beside the chair, not just hear about them.

The exam — 25 minutes

Tooth-by-tooth charting, gum measurements, bite and jaw check, and an oral cancer screening. This is also when we answer the saved-up questions.

Your cleaning — 45 minutes

Unless we find active gum disease, in which case we will explain why a different kind of appointment is the right next step.

The plan, in writing

Priorities, options, and the cost of each with your insurance applied. Printed, reviewed at the desk, and emailed to you as well.

Insurance & payment

No surprise bills

We are in network with Delta Dental Premier, Regence, MODA, Cigna and Pacific Source, and we file claims for every other PPO plan. Our treatment coordinator verifies your benefits before the first visit, so the estimate you sign is what you pay.

No dental insurance? Our in-house membership covers two cleanings, exams, x-rays and 20% off any treatment for a flat annual fee — no deductibles, no annual maximum, no claim forms.

Questions

About treatment

Do you refer out for root canals?

We treat front teeth and bicuspids in house. Molars, retreatments and anything with unusual anatomy go to an endodontist two blocks away — not because it is more profitable, but because they do twenty a week and we do two.

How long does a same-day crown really take?

Plan on two to two and a half hours from sitting down to walking out. The mill runs for about twenty minutes of that, and most patients read or watch something while it works.

Will whitening work on my teeth?

Usually, on natural enamel. It does not change the colour of crowns, veneers or composite fillings, which matters if you have front-tooth restorations — we will plan the order of treatment around that so nothing ends up mismatched.

What if treatment costs more than I can manage right now?

We sequence by urgency and spread it across months or insurance years, and we will tell you plainly what is safe to wait on. Interest-free payment plans through a third-party lender are available for larger cases.

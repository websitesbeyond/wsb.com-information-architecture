How we practice

A calmer kind of dental visit

We built the practice around the three complaints we heard most often from new patients.

Nothing rushed

Hygiene visits are scheduled for forty-five minutes because that is how long careful work takes. You will not be handed off mid-appointment.

Gentle by default

Topical numbing before every injection, warm blankets, noise-cancelling headphones, and a hand signal that stops everything immediately.

Priced before we start

A printed estimate with your insurance already applied, reviewed at the front desk before any treatment is scheduled.

Questions

Before you come in

Do you take my insurance?

We are in network with Delta Dental Premier, Regence, MODA, Cigna and Pacific Source, and we file claims for every other PPO plan as an out-of-network provider. Our coordinator will run your benefits before the first visit so the estimate you see is the number you pay.

What if I have not been to a dentist in years?

Then you are in good company — it is roughly a third of our new patients. The first visit is an exam and a conversation, not a lecture, and we will sequence treatment over months if that makes it manageable. Nobody here is going to make you feel bad about the gap.

Can you see my whole family on one morning?

Yes. We hold Wednesday and Friday mornings for family blocks and can run two chairs at once, so four cleanings take about two hours instead of four separate trips. Ask the front desk for a family block when you call.

What are my options if I am anxious?

Topical numbing before every injection, nitrous oxide if you want it, warm blankets, headphones, and a stop signal we honour immediately. For longer procedures we can prescribe oral sedation with a driver arranged in advance.

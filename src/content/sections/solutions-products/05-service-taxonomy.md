PLACEHOLDER — the engine the rest of the platform reads from. A canonical set of service terms that resolves related content by specificity: a service page looks for the most specific matching promos and testimonials, then widens to the service line if it finds none.

It also builds the internal linking between related services automatically, and drives schema markup — the parts of SEO that otherwise get maintained by hand and rot.
